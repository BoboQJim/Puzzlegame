SparrowElimination Strategy Guide: Master Mahjong Sliding, Make 10 Mode & Boosters

Published: 3 August 2026 Category: Mahjong Tile Sliding & Math Puzzle

Mahjong-inspired puzzle games have long captivated players worldwide with their perfect balance of cultural charm, tactile tile aesthetics, and deep strategic satisfaction. SparrowElimination takes this classic genre into exciting new territory by combining traditional Eastern tile symbols with dynamic sliding mechanics, spatial line-of-sight clearing, and an ingenious mathematical twist known as "Make 10 Mode."

Instead of merely matching static open tiles on a 3D stack like standard Mahjong Solitaire, SparrowElimination challenges your spatial awareness on a dynamic grid board. You must actively slide tiles horizontally or vertically across open pathways to line up matching pairs or calculate exact numeric sums. Whether you are aiming to conquer complex puzzle stages, clear high-pressure challenge levels, or master mathematical tile combinations, this comprehensive strategy guide covers every rule, power-up, and tactical secret you need to become an expert tile eliminator.

Core Gameplay Mechanics: Sliding & Unobstructed Line-of-Sight Alignment

At the heart of SparrowElimination lies a smooth directional sliding mechanic paired with a strict line-of-sight rule. The game grid is filled with traditional Mahjong tiles featuring bamboo suits, character numbers, dots, and honor tiles such as Winds and Dragons. Your primary objective across all game modes is simple: systematically clear every single tile off the board.

Tiles can be matched and eliminated under two specific operational conditions:

  • Direct Line-of-Sight Alignment: If two identical tiles share the exact same horizontal row or vertical column and there are no obstructing tiles located between them, clicking either tile instantly matches and clears the pair.
  • Dynamic Path Sliding: If two matching tiles are separated or offset on the board, you can click and drag a tile along open grid spaces horizontally or vertically. Sliding a tile until it sits directly adjacent to or in an open line with its matching counterpart allows you to complete the match and clear both tiles from the board.

Because every slide shifts spatial relationships across the grid, a single movement can either open up critical sightlines or accidentally block surrounding tiles. Careful visual planning before executing a slide is essential to avoiding deadlocks.

Exploring Game Modes: Make 10, Level Mode & Challenge Mode

To keep the gameplay fresh and intellectually engaging, SparrowElimination offers three distinct game modes tailored to different puzzle-solving styles.

1. Make 10 Mode (Math & Color Puzzle)

The Make 10 Mode introduces an additive mathematical mechanic into the traditional tile-matching formula. In this mode, tiles display numeric values alongside distinct color themes. Rather than simply matching identical numbers, your goal is to find two tiles of the same color whose numerical values sum up to exactly 10 (e.g., a Red 4 and a Red 6, or a Green 3 and a Green 7).

Special non-numbered honor tiles—such as the White Dragon (Bai Ban) or East Wind (Dong Feng)—do not carry numeric values. To eliminate these non-digit characters in Make 10 Mode, you follow classical Mahjong rules: pair two identical character tiles together to clear them from the board. This dual-rule dynamic requires players to constantly switch between rapid mental arithmetic and classic pattern recognition.

2. Level Mode (Progressive Puzzle Stages)

Level Mode serves as the core campaign of the game. Stages start with clean, spacious tile arrangements that introduce basic line-of-sight clears and simple slides. As you advance into higher levels, the boards become increasingly dense, featuring maze-like tile arrangements and narrow grid corridors.

To successfully pass a level, you must completely empty the board without running out of valid moves. Studying the outer edges of the board and systematically working your way inward is often the most effective tactic for maintaining open sliding channels.

3. Challenge Mode (High-Intensity Mastery)

Designed for seasoned puzzle enthusiasts, Challenge Mode tests your spatial problem-solving under maximum constraints. It utilizes the same core line-of-sight and sliding rules as Level Mode, but features tighter board configurations, higher tile diversity, and complex blocking patterns. Precision planning and strategic item usage are mandatory if you hope to achieve complete board clearances in Challenge Mode.

Mastering In-Game Power-Ups: Tactical Breakdown

When tiles become deeply locked or no clear line-of-sight connections remain on the grid, SparrowElimination provides three powerful utility items to assist your strategy. Using these tools at pivotal moments can save a challenging run from failure.

1. The Refresh Booster: Board Position Resets

When you encounter a completely deadlocked board where no matching pairs have clear sliding pathways or open line-of-sight alignment, activating the Refresh Booster shuffles and redistributes all remaining tiles across the existing grid spaces.

Refresh does not alter tile counts or remove any pieces; it simply rearranges their spatial distribution. This instantly breaks up cluster deadlocks and creates brand-new horizontal and vertical line-of-sight opportunities across the board.

2. The Remove Booster: Direct Tile Vaporization

The Remove Booster is your most potent tactical lifeline. When triggered, it automatically selects and clears one valid matching pair (or valid Make 10 combination) directly off the board, completely bypassing spatial barriers or blocking obstacles.

The Remove item is best reserved for eliminating isolated tiles that are trapped behind long walls of obstacles, or for removing troublesome key tiles that block multiple critical sliding paths through the center of the board.

3. The Tips Booster: Visual Pathway Highlighting

On densely packed boards, it can be easy to overlook subtle sliding opportunities or clear line-of-sight alignments. Activating the Tips Booster instantly highlights a pair of tiles that can be successfully slid and matched right away.

Tips serves as an incredible learning tool for training your visual perception, helping you spot tricky perimeter slides and complex multi-step moves that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Pro Player Strategies: How to Consistently Clear Dense Boards

To help you conquer tricky stages in both Level Mode and Challenge Mode, here are proven tactical recommendations from top players:

  • Clear Center Corridors Early: Focus initial moves on clearing tiles from the center of the grid. Opening up central rows and columns creates spacious corridors through which other tiles can freely slide across the board.
  • Scan Rows Before Column Sliding: Always examine both horizontal and vertical axes before moving a tile. Ensure a slide won't block a separate, high-priority match in an adjacent row.
  • Prioritize Non-Numbered Tiles in Make 10: Because non-digit character tiles (like White Dragon or East Wind) require exact pair matching rather than mathematical additions, clear them as soon as they align to reduce board clutter.
  • Save Remove Boosters for Bottlenecks: Resist the urge to use the Remove booster on easy matches. Save it for instances where a single stubborn tile traps several underlying pieces.
  • Plan Sliding Sequences in Advance: Treat the game board like a chess match. Think two or three slides ahead, confirming that your target tile will have an open exit route before initiating a move.
